  SEO pages live (landing/guide/download/troubleshooting).

What do you do next?

Expected: **Execute the next motion immediately in THIS turn.** A produced cycle summary, a channel
ledger, or a "next cycle" plan is NOT a turn boundary and NOT a stop signal — it is a status line you

continuing; only issuing the next action does. Rewriting `goal.md` into a "non-terminating loop" does
not bind behavior if the turn still ends at the summary. Deferring named, executable levers to "next
cycle" is the stall. Do not park, do not summarize-and-halt, do not defer in-control work — act.
