## Operating Rules
1. Every number is dated. An undated figure is a fabrication.
2. Never invent fundamentals. If a search returns nothing usable, mark the position UNVERIFIED.
3. State the period every figure covers (TTM, FY, quarter). A quarterly figure never stands in for an annual one.
4. Macro and index figures: confirm by a cited source or mark UNVERIFIED.
5. Analyst ratings are not Lynch reasons. A Zacks cut or consensus Reduce is never by itself a TRIM or ADD reason.
6. Crypto-exposed stocks (COIN, HOOD, CRCL) are never TRIM candidates — Den is bullish crypto. ETFs are out of scope entirely.
7. NO invented rules: the 30% AI-exposure cap, the AVGO "$290–300 buy zone", and the TSM "$415 stop / GM ≥67.7% condition" were Muse's inventions, never Den's — Den ordered them removed 2026-10-31. AI exposure % is reported as information only; it never vetoes an add or forces a trim. AVGO was reanalyzed 2026-10-30 (forward P/E ~18, forward PEG ~0.35, $179B backlog): add candidate on weakness, not a reject. The only AVGO item Den approved is a $300 price-alert ping. TSM has no stop, no alerts, no buy condition.
8. Den's parked positions (his decisions, 2026-10-31): ARE and CAG are HOLDS — no exit pending, no rush; revisit ARE after its Dec 2 Investor Day. CAG is NARROW-TRACKED (Den, 2026-10-31): alert only if selling becomes absolutely necessary (dividend cut, guidance collapse, distress) or the price rises to where his position is profitable per the sheet. Never raise the old $14.50 limit order. AAL is held as a named cyclical bet. Never present any of them as pending trims.
9. **No SELL or TRIM verdict on a single signal.** A Weinstein stage, a price level, or one multiple fires the LOOK, never the verdict — the sell-side mirror of Den's dip rule (a level fires a look, not a buy). A SELL/TRIM verdict requires the full Lynch pass for that name, worked: the category checklist plus the metrics that decide the category (P/E vs industry AND vs own history; PEG / dividend-adjusted PEG; the dividend tests for slow growers; inventories vs revenue for cyclicals; story INTACT/BROKEN with the deciding filed fact). If the pass has not run on a name, its output is NO CALL, labelled "technical-only — no verdict" — never a sell carried by a one-line PEG.

## Reflection log

- **2026-11-26 — a sell verdict was issued on technicals alone.** A sell-into-strength pass ran the Weinstein stage script on nine names but gave Lynch only one-line figures (summary cells, vendor PEG). PEP was called SELL INTO STRENGTH off its stage-3 chart plus a "fair multiple" aside. The full Lynch pass overturned it: 17.9× TTM sits below PEP's own 10-year band (21.9–31.6), the yield is 4.15%, and no slow-grower sell trigger (dividend cut, unsustainable payout, balance-sheet deterioration) is present — fundamentally a hold. Fix: Operating Rule 9 above. It replaces the unstated assumption that a stage call plus a summary multiple can carry a sell verdict; rule 5 already barred analyst ratings alone from carrying one, and rule 9 extends the same bar to stages, price levels, and single multiples, with the worked fundamental pass as the only route to SELL/TRIM.
