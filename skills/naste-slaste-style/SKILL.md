# naste-slaste-style — build, review, and improve a post like @naste_slaste

Use this skill to turn one real story into a Russian-language
Instagram Reel in the style of Anastasiia Bazueva (@naste_slaste):
a blunt, first-person founder monologue, not a tutorial and not
a motivational post.

Output language is Russian, unless the user asks otherwise.
English appears only as brand words, never as the whole post.

## 1. Build — in this order, no skipping

### 1a. Collect the input (ask, don't invent)

Get these five things before writing anything. If one is missing,
ask for it. Never invent dialogue, people, or facts to fill a gap.

1. Trigger: what actually happened, in one scene?
2. Dialogue: one real line someone said, or the user said.
3. Feeling: one honest sentence about how it felt.
4. Tension: what looks good from outside vs. what it costs inside?
5. Audience: who is this for, in one phrase?

Write the tension down first, in this exact form:

```
Внешне: [looks successful]
Внутри: [what it actually costs / feels like]
```

No tension pair = no post in this style. A story that is only
"I shipped a feature" needs the feeling underneath it first
(relief, fear, loneliness after the launch) or it does not fit.

### 1b. Hook — one line on screen, 4–8 words

Rules: a paradox or provocation, not a summary. Readable muted,
on a phone, in under 1 second. No greeting, no name, no logo.

Test it: if a stranger cannot argue with it or recognise
themselves in it instantly, rewrite it.

Original examples (patterns, not her words):

- "Сильная — значит, о тебе не заботятся?"
- "Успех есть. А дома — тишина."
- "IT girl или girl IT?"

Weak → strong:

- Weak: "Мои мысли о силе и отношениях" (a topic, not a hook)
- Strong: "Сильная — значит, о тебе не заботятся?"

### 1c. Reel script — 30–60 seconds, five beats

Write to this skeleton. One idea per beat, short sentences,
first person, present or recent past. The admission is the post;
give it the most time.

| Beat | Time | Write this |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | Hook on screen + first spoken line, mid-story. No intro. |
| Trigger | 3–15s | The one scene: who, where, what was said. Concrete detail only. |
| Admission | 15–35s | "Сначала я думала X, а потом заметила Y." The twist the user first denied. Do not rush this. |
| Widening | 35–50s | One line: why this happens to people like us, not just to me. No lecture, no list. |
| Question | 50s–end | One specific question the audience can answer from their own life. End here, not on a moral. |

Template to fill in:

```
[Hook on screen]

[Trigger: "Сегодня / вчера … сказал(а) …"]
[Admission: "Сначала я думала …, а потом поняла …"]
[Widening: "И дело не только во мне — …"]
[Question: "А у вас …?"]
```

### 1d. Caption — same story, three blocks

The caption must not tell a different story from the video.

```
[Block 1: the trigger, mid-story. No greeting.]
[Block 2: the admission and twist, 1–2 short paragraphs.]
[Block 3: the same specific question as the Reel.]
```

Rules: no tips list, no "5 советов", no corporate CTA, no
generic ending ("всё будет хорошо", "верьте в себя").
Hashtags: default to none. Add at most 2–3 only if the user
asks for discoverability, never on the first line.

### 1e. Visual brief — attach to every draft

- Vertical Reel, founder in frame: selfie or mirror selfie,
  phone in hand is fine.
- Setting: home, car, mirror, night street. Intimate, not a studio.
- Light: flash / night, high contrast, dark background, face lit.
- Text: the hook only, large bold Russian, white or red on
  dark, max 2 lines. Do not subtitle the whole monologue.
- Carousel variant, only with real photos: cover = hook poster,
  slides = editorial shots from one shoot, last slide = the
  question. Never an infographic or tutorial deck.

## 2. Review — score the draft before delivering it

Score every draft against this checklist. Pass = 8/10 or more,
AND no red flag. If it fails, do not deliver it as finished —
go to section 3 first.

- [ ] 1. Real trigger: a concrete scene and a real line, not a thesis.
- [ ] 2. Tension pair: внешне / внутри is written down and visible.
- [ ] 3. Hook: 4–8 words, paradox/provocation, works muted in 1 second.
- [ ] 4. Admission: a "first I thought X, then I noticed Y" turn exists.
- [ ] 5. No lecture: no tips list, no "5 lessons", no moral at the end.
- [ ] 6. Question: one specific question, answerable from the
      audience's own life — not "что думаете?".
- [ ] 7. Voice: Russian, first person, short sentences, one raw
      detail. Not polished, not corporate.
- [ ] 8. Caption = video: same trigger, same admission, same question.
- [ ] 9. Visual: founder in frame, night/flash, hook text only.
- [ ] 10. Honesty: every fact is the user's. Nothing invented.

Red flags — any one of these fails the draft on its own,
whatever the score:

- Invented dialogue, person, number, or event.
- A food, recipe, or how-to post (wrong model for this style).
- An English-language post, or a hook that only makes sense
  after reading the caption.

Write the review as: score, the failed checks by number, and
the single biggest weakness in one sentence. Then fix it.

## 3. Improve — fix by failure, not by rewriting blindly

Apply only the fixes for the checks that failed, in this order.
Re-score after each pass. Stop at 8/10 with no red flags; do
not polish past that into a different voice.

| Failed check | Exact fix |
|---|---|
| 1. No real trigger | Cut the opening thesis. Start mid-scene: place, person, line said. Ask the user for the missing detail if you do not have it. |
| 2. No tension | Write внешне / внутри before touching the script again. If both sides are the same, the story is not ready. |
| 3. Weak hook | Delete summary words ("мысли о", "история про"). Keep the paradox, cut to 8 words max. Test it muted in 1 second. |
| 4. No admission | Add one sentence: "Сначала я думала X, а потом заметила Y." Move it to the centre of the script and give it time. |
| 5. Lecture / moral | Delete the lesson and the ending moral. Replace the ending with the specific question. |
| 6. Vague question | Replace "что думаете?" with a question about a concrete choice or moment: "А у вас было, что …?" |
| 7. Too polished | Add one concrete detail and one raw sentence. Cut adjectives and corporate words ("благодарна команде", "удивительный опыт"). |
| 8. Caption ≠ video | Rewrite the caption from the script beats, in order. Do not write a second, better story in the caption. |
| 9. Generic visual | Change the brief to mirror/night/flash with the founder in frame. Delete stock or faceless graphics. |
| 10. Invented fact | Delete it, even if it makes the post weaker. A weaker true post beats a stronger invented one. |

### Worked example — weak draft, reviewed, improved

Weak v1 (fails checks 1, 3, 4, 5, 6):

> "Всем привет! Хочу поделиться 5 уроками о силе и успехе.
> Главное — верить в себя, и всё будет хорошо. Что думаете?"

Review: 2/10. Biggest weakness: no scene, no admission, and
it ends on a lecture plus a generic question.

Improved v2 (same topic, real shape — replace facts with the
user's own before delivering):

- Hook on screen: "Успех есть. А дома — тишина."
- Script: "Сегодня закрыла сделку, о которой мечтала полгода.
  Приехала домой, открыла дверь — и тишина. Сначала я думала,
  что мне просто нужно больше работать. А потом поняла: мне
  не с кем это разделить прямо сейчас, потому что я всё время
  'сильная и занятая'. И вот вопрос: успех считается, если
  его некому рассказать вечером?"
- Caption: same three beats, ending on that question.
- Visual: night mirror selfie, flash, phone in hand, hook
  in white on black.

Re-score v2: hook, admission, question and voice pass; it
ships only once every fact in it is the user's real one.

## 4. Deliver

Deliver as one package: tension pair, hook, Reel script,
caption, and visual brief — plus the review score and any
failed check you could not fix, stated plainly.

Never post under the user's identity without their explicit
go-ahead for that specific post. Before promising an Instagram
post, check whether their account is actually linked; if it
is not, hand over the package for manual upload.
